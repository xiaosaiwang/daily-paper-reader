## 摘要
理解生态适应的遗传基础是进化生物学的一个基本挑战。螺旋体门是一个古老的细菌门，具有异常广泛的生态范围，包括主要的人类病原体，如梅毒、莱姆病和钩端螺旋体病的病原体，以及来自不同环境的自由生活物种和最近描述的缺乏该门典型螺旋形态的物种。在这里，利用代表所有可培养螺旋体物种的整理基因组数据集，我们显示功能基因组架构（定义为编码能力在生物过程中的比例分配）与螺旋体门中的生态生活方式和表型特征相关，独立于系统发育关系，来自不同进化起源的宿主依赖谱系在共享功能图谱上趋同。门范围的系统基因组学分析将短螺旋体属确定为最早分化的谱系，重新审视了该门的进化根源，并确定螺旋形态是祖先特征，并在一次演化转变中丢失，该转变也与协调的功能变化相关。最后，祖先基因组重建揭示了共同祖先是一种能运动的、异养的、螺旋形细菌，具有先前未描述的代谢和结构特征。总之，这些结果为螺旋体门提供了一个整合的功能框架，说明了如何利用基因组架构来追踪一个广泛分布的细菌门的生态多样化。

## Abstract
Understanding the genetic basis of ecological adaptation is a fundamental challenge of evolutionary biology. Spirochaetes are an ancient bacterial phylum of exceptional ecological breadth, encompassing major human pathogens such as the causative agents of syphilis, Lyme disease, and leptospirosis, alongside free-living species from contrasting environments and recently described species that lack the canonical spiral morphology of the phylum. Here, using a curated dataset of genomes representing all cultivable spirochete species, we show that functional genome architecture (defined as the proportional allocation of coding capacity across biological processes) correlates with ecological lifestyle and phenotypic traits in Spirochaetes independently of phylogenetic relationships, with host-dependent lineages from distinct evolutionary origins converging on shared functional profiles. Phylum-wide phylogenomic analyses identified Brachyspira as the earliest-diverging lineage, revisiting the evolutionary rooting of the phylum, and establishing that the spiral morphology is ancestral and was lost in a single evolutionary transition also associated with coordinated functional changes. Lastly, ancestral genome reconstruction uncovers the common ancestor as a motile, heterotrophic, and spiral-shaped bacterium, with metabolic and structural features not previously described. Together, these results provide an integrated and functional framework for Spirochaetes, illustrating how genome architecture can be used to track the ecological diversification of a widespread bacterial phylum.
