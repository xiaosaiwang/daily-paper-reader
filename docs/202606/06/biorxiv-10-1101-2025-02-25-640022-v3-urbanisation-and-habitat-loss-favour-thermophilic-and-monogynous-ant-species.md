## 摘要
城市化与栖息地破碎化等环境变化通过改变栖息地、资源和微气候深刻影响生态群落。虽然栖息地破碎化和栖息地丧失被认为是塑造生物多样性的已知驱动因素，但它们对从物种分选到质量效应等元群落动态的影响仍存在争议。蚂蚁具有多样的生活史和强大的生态效应，是研究这些压力的理想模式物种。我们研究了巴黎地区蚂蚁群落（包括分类和功能多样性）对城市化和栖息地破碎化的响应，比较了城市中25个城市公园与城外24个乡村森林中的林地。我们发现城市和乡村环境之间的物种组成存在明显差异，城市中喜热性物种更常见，且倾向单后制。森林群落在我们所研究的面积水平上同质，而公园群落因公园面积不同而有显著差异，较大的公园拥有更多物种。我们的发现表明，城市化选择了特定的蚂蚁性状，并有利于更喜热的物种，从而提高了城市群落的平均热偏好。这些选择效应影响了哪些物种能够在不同斑块中定殖和生存，塑造了元群落结构，并可能影响气候变化下蚂蚁群落的恢复力。

## Abstract
Environmental changes such as urbanisation and habitat fragmentation profoundly impact ecological communities by altering habitats, resources, and microclimates. While habitat fragmentation and habitat loss are known drivers to shape biodiversity, their impacts on metacommunity dynamics, ranging from species sorting to mass effects, remain a subject of debate. Ants, with diverse life histories and strong ecological effects, are ideal model species to study these pressures. We investigated the response of ant communities, including taxonomic and functional diversity, to urbanisation and habitat fragmentation in the Paris region, comparing wooded areas in 25 urban parks vs 24 rural forests outside the city. We found a clear difference in species composition between urban and rural environments, with a higher prevalence of thermophilic species and a tendency for monogyny in the city. Forest communities are homogeneous across the size levels we studied, while park communities differ noticeably depending on park size, with larger parks harbouring more species. Our findings suggest that urbanisation selects specific ant traits and favours more thermophilic species, thereby increasing the mean thermal preference of urban communities. These selective effects influence which species can colonise and survive in different patches, shaping metacommunity structure and potentially affecting the resilience of ant communities under climate change.
