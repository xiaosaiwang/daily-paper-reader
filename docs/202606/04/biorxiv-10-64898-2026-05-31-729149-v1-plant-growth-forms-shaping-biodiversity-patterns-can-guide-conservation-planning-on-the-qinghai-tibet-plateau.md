## 摘要
植物生长型深刻反映了支撑生态系统功能的进化适应性，然而其生物地理差异如何影响保护优先级的确定仍鲜为人知。我们整合了青藏高原14,468种维管植物的系统发育树和高分辨率分布数据，发现生长型身份解耦了分类多样性和系统发育多样性的空间格局，不同生长型在地理分布范围和海拔最适带（2,200-3,300米）上表现出显著差异。尽管草本植物在空间尺度上主导植物群落，但其相对丰度沿西北至东南干旱梯度递减，在湿润、物种丰富的区域被结构复杂的非草本生长型所取代。因此，针对单个生长型确定的优先保护区空间相似性很低。尽管存在这种差异，我们识别出乔木和藤本植物是高效的伞护生长型：优先保护这些功能群可以在最小空间范围内捕获其他所有生长型的多维多样性≥75%。我们的研究表明，考虑基于生长型的功能身份对于最大化保护效率至关重要，为全球生物多样性热点提供了可扩展的框架。

## Abstract
Plant growth forms effectively reflect profound evolutionary adaptations that underpin ecosystem functioning; however, how their biogeographic disparities influence conservation prioritization remains poorly understood. We here integrate a comprehensive phylogeny with high-resolution distribution data for 14,468 vascular plant species across the Qinghai-Tibet Plateau, showing that growth-form identity decouples the spatial patterns of taxonomic and phylogenetic diversity, with distinct forms exhibiting marked differences in geographic range sizes and elevational optima (2,200-3,300 m). Although herbs dominate plant assemblages across spatial scales, their proportional abundance decreases along a northwest-to-southeast aridity gradient, yielding to structurally complex, non-herbaceous forms in humid, species-rich regions. Consequently, priority conservation areas derived for individual growth forms exhibit low spatial similarity. Despite this divergence, we identify trees and climbers as highly effective umbrella growth forms: prioritizing these functional groups captures [&ge;]75% of the multidimensional diversity of all other growth forms within a minimal spatial footprint. Our findings demonstrate that accounting for growth-form-based functional identity is essential for maximizing conservation efficacy, providing a scalable framework for biodiversity hotspots worldwide.
