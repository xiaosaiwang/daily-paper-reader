## 摘要
干旱威胁全球粮食安全。适应性根系表型和微生物组可以改善玉米（Zea mays L.）的水分吸收和耐旱性。然而，根系表型与微生物组之间的协同作用仍未得到充分探索。我们旨在研究田间尺度干旱条件下不同根系表型与根际微生物组之间的关联。我们在正常水分条件和通过遮雨棚排除降雨造成的干旱条件下，田间种植了22个玉米自交系。我们量化了籽粒产量，并分别通过激光烧蚀断层扫描获得的根冠和横截面图像测量了根系结构和解剖表型。我们通过核糖体标记的DNA元条形码技术表征了根际原核生物和真菌群落。根际微生物多样性主要与根系解剖结构相关，而非根系结构。在对照条件下，皮层薄壁细胞壁宽度解释了原核生物β多样性的13.1%的变异，并与籽粒产量相关。在相同条件下，皮层细胞列数和后生木质部导管数解释了原核生物和真菌β多样性变异的1.4-2.1%。在干旱条件下未观察到根系表型的影响。我们发现微生物分类群丰度与根系解剖表型之间存在248个显著相关性，尤其是与皮层相关的表型，如细胞列数和活皮层面积。总体而言，在对照条件下发现了更多的相关性。我们确定了能够解释微生物β多样性变异的小但显著比例的根系表型，主要是在正常水分条件下。我们表明，特别是根系解剖结构与田间玉米根际微生物多样性相关。

## Abstract
Drought threatens food security globally. Adaptive root phenotypes and microbiomes can improve maize (Zea mays L.) water uptake and tolerance to drought. However, synergisms between root phenotypes and microbiomes remain underexplored. We aimed to investigate the association between varying root phenotypes and rhizosphere microbiomes under field-scale drought. We grew 22 maize inbred lines in the field under optimal water availability and drought imposed by excluding rain with rainout shelters. We quantified grain yield and measured root architectural and anatomical phenotypes on root crown and cross-section images obtained by laser ablation tomography, respectively. We characterized rhizosphere prokaryotic and fungal communities with DNA metabarcoding of ribosomal markers. Rhizosphere microbial diversity predominantly associated with root anatomy rather than root architecture. Cortical parenchyma wall width explained 13.1% of the variance of the prokaryotic {beta}-diversity and correlated with grain yield under control conditions. Under the same conditions, number of cortical cell files and metaxylem vessels explained 1.4-2.1% of the variance of prokaryotic and fungal {beta}-diversities. No effect of the root phenotypes was observed under drought. We found 248 significant correlations between microbial taxa abundances and root anatomical phenotypes, especially cortex-related phenotypes such as number of cell files and living cortical area. Overall, a greater number of correlations was found under control conditions. We identified root phenotypes explaining a small but significant percentage of the variance of the microbial {beta}-diversity, mostly under optimal water availability. We showed that especially root anatomy is associated with rhizosphere microbial diversity in field-grown maize.
