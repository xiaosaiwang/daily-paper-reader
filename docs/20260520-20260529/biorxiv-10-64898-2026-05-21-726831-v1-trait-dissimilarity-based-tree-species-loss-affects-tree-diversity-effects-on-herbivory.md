## 摘要
生物多样性丧失不仅可以通过改变树种丰富度，还通过从生态群落中丧失特定功能策略来改变相互作用。在亚热带森林多样性实验中，我们探究了树种丰富度对节肢动物植食性和叶部病原侵染的影响是否依赖于群落功能多样性，以及基于性状差异性的非随机物种丧失与随机丧失相比是否会改变这些关系。为此，我们结合了已建立的种植情景与新构建的灭绝路径。我们测试了植食性和叶部病原侵染对（i）树种丰富度、功能多样性（雷氏Q）、群落结构和资源策略（即常绿性）以及群落加权性状均值、捕食作用的响应，以及（ii）基于性状差异性的灭绝路径分析，该分析对比了功能相似与功能不同树种的定向丧失。植食性随树种丰富度增加而增加，并且在树木功能多样性较高的群落中，这种增加显著更强。在定向物种丧失情景下，当相似树种首先丧失时，植食性与随机丧失预期的差异最大。相反，首先丧失功能不同的物种所产生的丰富度效应更接近随机丧失情景。因此，基于性状的物种丧失将比随机丧失更强烈地改变营养相互作用。对于病原侵染，树种丰富度效应取决于常绿性和种植灭绝情景（三向交互作用），基于性状的灭绝路径与随机丧失预期仅有微小偏差。病原侵染也倾向于随群落加权平均叶片氮含量增加而增加。捕食作用与树种丰富度或功能多样性无明显关系，但与植食性正相关。这种关联的强度在不同灭绝情景间存在差异，未提供一致的自上而下调控的证据。

综合：生物多样性丧失对叶片损伤的生态后果取决于哪些功能策略丧失，而不仅仅取决于剩余多少树种。通过将观测到的树木多样性梯度与基于性状的灭绝路径相结合，本研究表明功能多样性和宿主冗余有助于解释为什么植食者和病原体通过不同的功能约束受到相同的树木多样性变化的影响，并提高了在非随机物种丧失下对相互作用强度的预测。

O_LIBiodiversity loss can alter interactions not only through changes in tree species richness, but also through the loss of particular functional strategies from ecological communities. Working in a subtropical forest diversity experiment we asked whether tree species richness effects on arthropod herbivory and leaf pathogen infestation depend on community functional diversity, and whether trait dissimilarity-based, non-random species loss alters these relationships compared to random loss. To address this, we combined already established planted scenarios with newly constructed extinction pathways.
C_LIO_LIWe tested the responses of herbivory and leaf pathogen infestation (i) to tree species richness, functional diversity (Raos Q), community structure and resource strategies (i.e. ever-greenness) and community-weighted trait means as well as predation, and (ii) trait dissimilarity-based extinction pathway analyses that contrasted directed loss of functionally similar versus functionally distinct tree species.
C_LIO_LIHerbivory increased with tree species richness and this increase was significantly stronger in communities with higher tree functional diversity. Under directed species loss scenarios, herbivory differed most strongly from random-loss expectations when similar tree species were lost first. By contrast, losing functionally distinct species first produced richness effects that were much closer to the random-loss scenarios. Trait-based species loss will therefore modify trophic interactions more strongly than random loss. For pathogen infestation tree richness effects depended on evergreenness and among planted extinction scenarios (three-way interaction), with only minor deviations of trait-based extinction path-ways from random-loss expectations. Pathogen infestation also tended to increase with community-weighted mean leaf nitrogen. Predation showed no clear relationship with tree species richness or functional diversity but was positively associated with herbivory. The strength of this association differed among extinction scenarios, providing no evidence for consistent top-down regulation.

SynthesisThe ecological consequences of biodiversity loss for leaf damage depend on which functional strategies are lost, not only on how many tree species remain.
