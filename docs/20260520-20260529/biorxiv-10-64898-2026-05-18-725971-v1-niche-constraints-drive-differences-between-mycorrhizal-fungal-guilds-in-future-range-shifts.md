## 摘要
菌根真菌是一类多样且普遍存在的关键植物共生体，其分布对全球生态系统功能有重要影响。然而，迄今为止，我们缺乏关于不同菌根真菌类群分布范围的定量数据，这制约了我们预测群落组成和功能未来变化的能力。本文利用121,079个DNA序列来源的观测数据，绘制了621种常见菌根真菌类群的分布图，并预测了其分布范围的未来变化。我们证明，气候和土壤因素，特别是年均温和土壤有机碳，对菌根真菌的分布具有主要控制作用，而丛枝菌根真菌和外生菌根真菌功能类群的生态位存在一致性差异。丛枝菌根真菌类群通常比外生菌根真菌占据更宽的生态位宽度，出现在更广泛的气候、土壤、植被覆盖、地形和干扰条件范围内。我们的模型还预测，在未来全球气候情景下，菌根真菌类群的分布范围将普遍缩小，平均范围减少12.6%或约750万平方公里。这种预测的分布范围减少在外生菌根真菌中最为显著，与其较小的总体生态位约束密切相关。通过生成常见菌根真菌及其相关环境生态位的全球图谱，我们为广泛怀疑的全球真菌生物多样性下降建立了关键基线。

## Abstract
Mycorrhizal fungi are a diverse and ubiquitous group of critical plant symbionts whose distribution strongly influences ecosystem function across the globe. Yet, until now, we do not have quantitative data on the range sizes of different mycorrhizal fungal taxa, limiting our capacity to forecast future shifts in community composition and function. Here, we use 121,079 DNA sequence-derived observations to map the distribution of 621 common mycorrhizal fungal taxa and forecast future changes to their range sizes. We demonstrate that climate and soil factors, particularly mean annual temperature and soil organic carbon, exert major control over mycorrhizal fungal distributions, and the ecological niches of mycorrhizal fungi consistently differ between arbuscular and ectomycorrhizal functional guilds. Arbuscular mycorrhizal fungal taxa generally occupy a wider niche breadth than ectomycorrhizal fungi, occurring across larger ranges of climate, soil, plant cover, topography, and disturbance conditions. Our models also predict widespread decreases in the range size of mycorrhizal fungal taxa under projected future global climates, with average ranges decreasing by 12.6% or [~]7.5 million km2. This decrease in projected range size will be most pronounced for ectomycorrhizal fungi, strongly linked to constraints from their smaller overall niches. By generating a global atlas of common mycorrhizal fungi and their associated environmental niche, we establish a critical baseline for widely suspected declines in global fungal biodiversity.
